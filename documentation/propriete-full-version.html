CIDOC CRM - Définitions (Page 1 - Verso)
P1 is identified by
QR
This property describes the naming or identification of any real-world item by a name or any other identifier. This property is intended for identifiers in general use, which form part of the world the CRM intends to describe, and not merely for internal database identifiers which are specific to a technical system, unless these latter also have a more general use outside the technical context.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P2 has type
QR
This property allows sub typing of CIDOC CRM entities – a form of specialisation – through the use of a terminological hierarchy, or thesaurus. The CIDOC CRM is intended to provide a common semantic framework and relationships needed to describe data structures. Consequently, it does not specialise entities any further than is required for this immediate purpose.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P4 has time-span
QR
This property associates an instance of E2 Temporal Entity with the instance of E52 Time-Span during which it was on-going. The associated instance of E52 Time-Span is understood as the real time-span during which the phenomena making up the temporal entity instance were active. More than one instance of E2 Temporal Entity may share a common instance of E52 Time-Span.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P7 took place at
QR
This property describes the spatial location of an instance of E4 Period. The related instance of E53 Place should be seen as a wider approximation of the geometric area within which the phenomena that characterise the period in question occurred. P7 took place at (witnessed) does not convey any meaning other than spatial positioning (frequently in a wider context).
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P14 carried out by
QR
This property describes the active participation of an instance of E39 Actor in an instance of E7 Activity. It implies causal or legal responsibility. The P14.1 in the role of property of the property specifies the nature of an Actor's participation.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P16 used specific object
QR
This property describes the use of material or immaterial things in a way essential to the performance of the outcome of an instance of E7 Activity. This property typically applies to tools, instruments, moulds, raw materials and items embedded in a product of the activity, or to the use of the object in question when necessary condition for the action.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P43 has dimension
QR
This property records an instance of E54 Dimension of some instance of E70 Thing. It is a shortcut of the more fully developed path from E70 Thing through P39 measured (was measured by), E16 Measurement P40 observed dimension (was observed in) to E54 Dimension. It offers no information about how and when an E54 Dimension was established.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P44 has condition
QR
This property links an instance of E18 Physical Thing to an instance of E3 Condition State that describes the condition of that physical thing at a particular time. The condition state describes the quality of the physical properties and characteristics of the object.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P45 consists of
QR
This property identifies the instances of E57 Material of which an instance of E18 Physical Thing is composed. The material of which an object is made is predominantly determined at the time of production. However, materials may be incorporated into or removed from an item at later times.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P46 is composed of
QR
This property associates an instance of E18 Physical Thing with another instance of E18 Physical Thing that forms part of it. The spatial extent of the composing part is included in the spatial extent of the whole. This property is transitive and asymmetric.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P48 has preferred identifier
QR
This property records the preferred instance of E42 Identifier that was used to identify an instance of E1 CRM Entity at the time this property was recorded. The fact that an identifier is a preferred one for an organisation can change over time. It does not imply that this identifier is superior to other identifiers in any semantic sense.
⚙
CIDOC CRM v.7.1.3
P67 refers to
QR
This property documents that an instance of E89 Propositional Object makes a statement about an instance of E1 CRM Entity. P67 refers to (is referred to by) has the property of referring to general concepts or entities that do not necessarily exist in the domain of discourse.
